What we do

Comprehensive Dental Care

A clinic equipped for endodontics first — and for everything that follows once a tooth is saved.

Root Canal Treatment

Single-visit where the tooth allows, under the operating microscope. Re-treatment of failed canals too.

Crowns & Bridges

Zirconia and metal-ceramic crowns that rebuild a tooth after a root canal, a fracture or heavy decay.

Dental Implants

For a tooth that genuinely cannot be saved — placement, healing and the final crown, planned end to end.

Extractions & Wisdom Teeth

Including impacted and surgically difficult wisdom teeth, with written aftercare you can actually follow.

Cleaning & Prevention

Scaling, polishing and a proper look at what is developing. The cheapest dentistry you will ever have.

Dental Emergencies

Meet your dentist

Dr. Amit Malik

MDS · Restorative Dentist & Endodontist

Nothing replaces a natural tooth as well as the natural tooth. An implant is good engineering and a crown is a good repair, but both are replacements — a tooth kept in the jaw keeps its own ligament, its own bone, and the feedback that tells you how hard you are biting.

Dr. Malik trained as an endodontist because that is the specialty whose entire purpose is keeping teeth other people would remove.

  • EndodonticsRoot canal treatment and re-treatment under magnification
  • RestorativeCrowns, bridges and rebuilding broken-down teeth
  • SurgicalExtractions, including impacted wisdom teeth
  • ImplantsReplacement where a tooth cannot be saved
