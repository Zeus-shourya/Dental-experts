Emergency Services

Severe pain, facial swelling, a knocked-out tooth or a front tooth broken in an accident are fitted in around booked appointments. Call rather than using the form.

While you wait

  • A cold compress on the outside of the cheek, not heat
  • Painkillers you normally tolerate, at the normal dose
  • Never put aspirin directly on the gum — it burns the tissue
  • A knocked-out adult tooth: hold it by the crown, keep it in milk
