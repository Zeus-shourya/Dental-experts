Our Treatments

Ten treatments offered at Sector 8, Sonipat. Prices are quoted after an examination — a number given over the phone, without seeing the tooth, is a guess.

What the clinic is built for

Specialist Treatments

These four are the core of the practice — confirmed by the clinic’s own description and its patient reviews.

01

Root Canal Treatment

When decay or a crack reaches the nerve, the tooth can be cleaned out and sealed instead of removed. Done here under an operating microscope, which is what makes narrow, curved and calcified canals findable in the first place.

What this covers

  • Single-visit treatment where the tooth allows it
  • Re-treatment of a root canal done elsewhere that has failed
  • Front teeth and molars, including difficult curved canals
  • The crown afterwards — a root-treated back tooth needs one
02

Crowns & Bridges

A cap that rebuilds a tooth too broken down to hold a filling — after a root canal, a fracture, or heavy decay. A bridge does the same across a gap, using the teeth on either side.

What this covers

  • Zirconia and metal-ceramic crowns
  • Post and core build-ups where little tooth structure is left
  • Bridges for a single missing tooth
  • Shade matching against your existing teeth
03

Dental Implants

A titanium root placed in the jaw, carrying a crown on top. The right answer for a tooth that genuinely cannot be saved — and only then, because nothing replaces a natural tooth as well as the natural tooth.

What this covers

  • Assessment of whether there is enough bone to hold one
  • Placement, the healing period, and the final crown
  • Single implants and multiple-tooth cases
  • An honest answer if a bridge would serve you better
04

Extractions & Wisdom Teeth

When a tooth really has to go — or when a wisdom tooth is impacted, repeatedly infected, or damaging the tooth in front of it.

What this covers

  • Routine extractions under local anaesthetic
  • Surgical removal of impacted wisdom teeth
  • Written aftercare instructions, not verbal ones you forget
  • A conversation about replacing the gap, before it closes badly

Also offered

General Dentistry

Everyday care alongside the specialist work.

05

Cleaning & Preventive Care

Scaling and polishing to remove the hardened deposit a brush cannot, plus a proper look at what is developing. The cheapest dentistry you will ever have is the appointment that stops you needing the expensive kind.

06

Gum Treatment

Bleeding, receding or loose-feeling gums. Deep cleaning below the gumline, and a plan to stop the bone loss that quietly loosens teeth long before they hurt.

07

Whitening & Smile Design

Professional whitening, and cosmetic work on front teeth where shape or spacing is the issue. Assessed first — staining from decay or an old filling does not respond to whitening.

08

Dentures

Complete and partial dentures, fitted and adjusted until they actually stay put. Repairs and relines for a set that has loosened over the years.

09

Children’s Dentistry

Check-ups, fillings, fluoride and sealants for milk teeth — which matter, because an infected milk tooth affects the adult tooth forming underneath it. Someone can stay with your child throughout.

10

Dental Emergencies

Severe pain, facial swelling, a knocked-out tooth, or a front tooth broken in an accident. Call rather than book online — these are fitted in, not scheduled.

In pain right now?

Call, do not wait for a form

If it is out of hours and a tooth has been knocked out, keep it in milk — not water — and get to a dentist the same day.

While you wait

  • A cold compress on the outside of the cheek, not heat
  • Painkillers you normally tolerate, at the normal dose
  • Do not put aspirin directly on the gum — it burns the tissue
  • A knocked-out adult tooth: hold it by the crown, never the root

Start With an Examination

Every treatment on this page begins the same way: someone looks at the tooth, takes an X-ray if one is needed, and tells you the options and what each costs. If you have been quoted elsewhere, bring the quote and the X-ray.
