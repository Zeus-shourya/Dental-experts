Our Treatments

Ten treatments offered at Sector 8, Sonipat. Prices are quoted after an examination — a number given over the phone, without seeing the tooth, is a guess.

What we treat

Specialist Treatments

Everything the clinic treats — from a routine clean to the specialist work it is built and equipped for.

01

Magnification-Aided Root Canal Treatment

When decay or a crack reaches the nerve, the tooth can be cleaned out and sealed instead of removed. Done here under an operating microscope, which is what makes narrow, curved and calcified canals findable in the first place.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Single-visit treatment where the tooth allows it
  • Re-treatment of a root canal done elsewhere that has failed
  • Front teeth and molars, including difficult curved canals
  • The crown afterwards — a root-treated back tooth needs one
02

Crowns & Bridges

A cap that rebuilds a tooth too broken down to hold a filling — after a root canal, a fracture, or heavy decay. A bridge does the same across a gap, using the teeth on either side.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Zirconia, E-max and metal-ceramic crowns
  • Post and core build-ups where little tooth structure is left
  • Bridges for a single missing tooth
  • Shade matching against your existing teeth
03

Dental Implants & Implant-Supported Prosthesis

A titanium root placed in the jaw, carrying a crown on top. The right answer for a tooth that genuinely cannot be saved — and only then, because nothing replaces a natural tooth as well as the natural tooth. Where several teeth or a whole arch are missing, implants can carry a fixed bridge or a secured denture instead of a crown on each one.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Assessment of whether there is enough bone to hold one
  • Placement, the healing period, and the final crown
  • Implant-supported bridges and dentures, up to a full arch
  • Single implants and multiple-tooth cases
  • An honest answer if a bridge would serve you better
04

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Rebuilding a whole mouth rather than one tooth — where years of wear, decay or missing teeth have left the bite collapsed and the teeth no longer working together. Planned as one sequence from the start, rather than as a series of unrelated appointments.

Book Now

What this covers

  • A full assessment of the bite, the joints and every remaining tooth
  • A written plan and running order agreed before anything starts
  • Root canals, crowns, bridges and implants combined into one result
  • Staged across visits, with the cost set out at the outset
05

Extractions & Wisdom Teeth

When a tooth really has to go — or when a wisdom tooth is impacted, repeatedly infected, or damaging the tooth in front of it.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Routine extractions under local anaesthetic
  • Surgical removal of impacted wisdom teeth
  • Written aftercare instructions, not verbal ones you forget
  • A conversation about replacing the gap, before it closes badly
06

Cleaning & Preventive Care

Scaling and polishing to remove the hardened deposit a brush cannot, plus a proper look at what is developing. The cheapest dentistry you will ever have is the appointment that stops you needing the expensive kind.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Scaling and polishing, above and below the gumline
  • Stain from tea, coffee and tobacco
  • A look at what is starting, while it is still small
  • Fluoride and sealants where they will do some good
07

Gum Treatment

Bleeding, receding or loose-feeling gums. Deep cleaning below the gumline, and a plan to stop the bone loss that quietly loosens teeth long before they hurt.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Deep cleaning of the root surfaces, under anaesthetic
  • Gum surgery where cleaning alone cannot reach
  • Pocket depths measured, so progress is a number
  • The home routine that keeps the result once it is won
08

Braces

Fixed braces to straighten crowded, crooked or protruding teeth and to correct a bite that does not meet properly. Suitable for teenagers and adults alike — how long it takes depends on what has to move.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Metal and ceramic fixed braces
  • Crowding, spacing, and bites that do not meet
  • Teenagers and adults alike
  • A timeline set at the start, and what happens if it slips
09

Invisalign

Clear removable aligners instead of fixed braces — near-invisible, and taken out to eat and to clean your teeth. They suit many cases but not every one; an assessment tells you which yours is.

Book Now

What this covers

  • A scan and a plan before you commit to anything
  • Removable aligners changed on a set schedule
  • Out to eat, out to clean your teeth
  • An honest answer when fixed braces would do it better
10

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening, stronger and better controlled than anything sold over the counter. Assessed first: staining that comes from decay or an old filling will not respond to whitening, and you should know that before you pay for it.

Book Now

What this covers

  • In-clinic whitening and take-home trays
  • The cause of the staining assessed first
  • Existing crowns and fillings will not change shade
  • What the result should be, before you pay for it
11

Smile Design

Reshaping the front teeth so they suit your face rather than a template — shape, length, spacing and shade planned together, using veneers, bonding or crowns as the case needs.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Shape, length, spacing and shade planned together
  • Veneers, bonding or crowns, as the case needs
  • A preview before anything is cut
  • A result that suits your face, not a template
12

Dentures

Complete and partial dentures, fitted and adjusted until they actually stay put. Repairs and relines for a set that has loosened over the years.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Complete and partial dentures
  • Adjusted until they stay put, not just until they fit
  • Repairs and relines for a set that has loosened
  • Implant-supported options where they are worth it
13

Children’s Dentistry

Check-ups, fillings, fluoride and sealants for milk teeth — which matter, because an infected milk tooth affects the adult tooth forming underneath it. Someone can stay with your child throughout.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Check-ups, fillings, fluoride and sealants for milk teeth
  • An infected milk tooth treated before it reaches the adult one
  • Someone can stay with your child throughout
  • A first visit that is not a filling
14

Dental Trauma Management

A tooth knocked out, pushed out of line or broken in a fall, a game or an accident. The first hour decides a great deal — a knocked-out adult tooth can often be put back if it is handled properly and you get here quickly.

Book Now

What this covers

  • A knocked-out adult tooth put back, where it still can be
  • Splinting a tooth that has been loosened or moved
  • Root canal treatment where the nerve has died
  • The follow-up that tells you whether the tooth has settled
15

24 Hrs Dental Emergencies

Severe pain, facial swelling, a knocked-out tooth, or a front tooth broken in an accident. Call rather than book online — these are fitted in, not scheduled.

Book Now

What this covers

  • Severe pain, facial swelling and dental abscesses
  • A knocked-out or broken front tooth
  • Fitted in around booked appointments
  • Call rather than fill in the form

In pain right now?

Call, do not wait for a form

If it is out of hours and a tooth has been knocked out, keep it in milk — not water — and get to a dentist the same day.

Call +91 90345 55897 Clinic location

While you wait

  • A cold compress on the outside of the cheek, not heat
  • Painkillers you normally tolerate, at the normal dose
  • Do not put aspirin directly on the gum — it burns the tissue
  • A knocked-out adult tooth: hold it by the crown, never the root

Start With an Examination

Every treatment on this page begins the same way: someone looks at the tooth, takes an X-ray if one is needed, and tells you the options and what each costs. If you have been quoted elsewhere, bring the quote and the X-ray.
